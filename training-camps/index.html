How it works

From request to training in six steps

  1. Send your request

    Fill in the form with your group, dates and needs.

  2. We check availability

    We check track time, El Cubo and every service you've asked for.

  3. You receive a tailored proposal

    Sessions, services and price, prepared for your group.

  4. Accept and pay the deposit

    Your booking is confirmed once the deposit is received and availability is approved.

  5. Pay the balance before arrival

    The remaining amount is paid before your camp starts.

  6. Train in Alicante

    Arrive and train. We coordinate the services included in your camp.

Payments are made by card through a secure payment link (Stripe).

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does sending a request commit us to anything?

No. The request lets us check availability and prepare a proposal. Your booking is only confirmed when you accept the proposal and the deposit is received.

Can we split our team into groups?

Yes, on request. If, for example, sprinters and endurance athletes need different sessions, tell us in the form and we'll plan time slots for each group, subject to availability.

Are the track time slots guaranteed?

No. They are indicative and always subject to availability. We check every request, and nothing is booked until we confirm it.

Do you provide accommodation?

We don't own any accommodation, but we can help you find hotels or apartments that suit your group.

How does payment work?

When you accept the proposal, you pay a deposit by card through a secure payment link (Stripe). The booking is confirmed once the deposit is received and availability is approved. The balance is paid before arrival.

How much does a camp cost?

It depends on the number of participants, dates, duration, facilities and services. You'll find the total in your proposal.

Which languages do you work in?

We communicate in English and Spanish.
